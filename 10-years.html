10 Years Unity Concert

Saturday, 31st October 2026

Date & Time

31 Oct 2026
10:00 AM - 6:00 PM

Venue

Kenyatta Stadium
Maralal, Samburu

Admission

FREE ENTRY
For Everyone

Walking Together For Peace

More than an entertainment event, the Unity Concert is a platform for peace, cultural appreciation, and youth empowerment. It brings together the Turkana and Samburu communities in a shared space for positive interaction.

The event will begin with a Peace Walk from Yare to Kenyatta Stadium, symbolizing the journey towards peaceful coexistence. We start together. We walk together. We arrive together. We celebrate together.
