Partnership & Sponsorship

Align your brand with a historic event promoting peace, unity, and cultural celebration in Northern Kenya.

Executive Summary

The Dufla Diligon 10 Years Anniversary Unity Concert is a landmark celebration of a decade of music, creativity and community connection. Scheduled for Saturday, 31st October 2026 at Kenyatta Stadium, Maralal, Samburu County, with free entry for everyone.

More than an entertainment event, the concert is designed as a platform for peace, unity, cultural appreciation and youth empowerment, bringing together the Turkana and Samburu communities and the general public in a shared space for positive interaction and celebration.

Why Free Entry? Leaving A Positive Footprint

We do not want the cost of a ticket to determine who gets to participate in a peace and unity initiative. Free Entry Means:

Accessibility & Inclusion

Everyone has an opportunity to attend. No community or economic group is left behind.

Interaction & Impact

Greater opportunity for Turkana, Samburu and other communities to mingle, ensuring the peace message reaches a wider audience.

Key Benefits To Sponsors

  • BRAND VISIBILITY - Prominent exposure through event branding, promotional materials, social media, radio, digital platforms and on-ground activations.
  • AUDIENCE ENGAGEMENT - Direct interaction with attendees through branded activations, product displays, sampling, competitions and demonstrations.
  • CSR & COMMUNITY IMPACT - Align your brand with a meaningful initiative promoting peace, unity, cultural appreciation and positive community relations.
  • AUDIENCE REACH - Connect with a diverse audience comprising youth, families, creatives, entrepreneurs, community leaders and residents from Samburu and surrounding regions.
  • YOUTH & TALENT EMPOWERMENT - Support emerging artists by providing a platform to showcase their talent alongside established performers.
  • CULTURAL CONNECTION - Associate your brand with a celebration of the rich cultural heritage and diversity of the Samburu and Turkana communities.
