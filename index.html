Biography

David Longoji
Ikiru

David Longoji Ikiru, popularly known as Dufla Diligon, is a Kenyan recording artist, performer, and songwriter whose music is deeply rooted in the culture, stories and lived experiences of Northern Kenya. Born and raised in Baragoi, Samburu County, Dufla blends Dancehall, Afrobeat, Reggae and contemporary African sounds.

Dufla gained national recognition with his hit single "Tempo", and most recently through his collaboration with Iyanii on "Donjo Maber" — named Citizen Digital's Song of the Year 2025.

Celebrating a decade in the music industry, Dufla continues to record, perform and collaborate across the region, championing the rich cultural identity of Northern Kenya.
